Clock
To sync: one phone taps "Be the reference clock" and turns its beacon on. Hold another phone a few cm from it and tap "Get the time from a nearby phone" (or shake both). Synced phones can beacon too, so the group doubles each round.

Loudness test (two synced phones a few cm apart)

A chirps on the tick, B three quarters of a period later. Each phone measures both chirps, so you see how loud the partner is compared with itself.
